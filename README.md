## About

Operations Research Analyst focused on **data-driven process optimization and automation**. I build and maintain Python/SQL data pipelines that turn operational and financial inputs into structured, validated datasets; produce decision-support analytics for planning and standardization; and integrate data across internal systems via APIs with monitoring and documentation.

My work applies graduate training in **Applied Machine Intelligence** — data engineering, ML evaluation, and analytics — to production business workflows in the commercial mechanical-contracting industry.
